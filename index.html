About Our Institution

CM SOE UPG HS Saraidih

Upgraded under the prestigious Chief Minister's School of Excellence (CM SOE) scheme by the Government of Jharkhand, our institution in Saraidih, Latehar bridges the gap between rural talent and modern educational standards.

We focus on activity-based learning, foundational literacy, vocational skills, and competitive readiness so every student thrives without financial barriers.

Our Vision

To transform rural education in Latehar by providing world-class, affordable, and holistic learning opportunities—empowering students from Saraidih to compete globally, think critically, and grow into responsible citizens.

"Excellence Without Barriers"

Our Mission

  • Academic Rigor: Delivering quality curriculum aligned with CBSE/JAC excellence standards.
  • Digital Inclusion: Hands-on learning through smart boards and modern ICT computer labs.
  • Holistic Growth: Nurturing sports, arts, leadership, and vocational readiness.
Campus Infrastructure

Modern Facilities for Students

Equipped as per CM School of Excellence benchmarks to foster curiosity, safety, and practical skills.

Smart Classrooms

Interactive digital panels and audio-visual learning tools that simplify complex concepts across all subjects.

ICT & Computer Lab

Dedicated computer workstations with broadband connectivity for coding, digital literacy, and e-learning.

Integrated Science Labs

Fully stocked Physics, Chemistry, and Biology laboratories encouraging experiential scientific inquiry.

Rich Library & Reading Room

Quiet reading spaces stocked with NCERT/CBSE textbooks, reference encyclopedias, newspapers, and magazines.

Sports & Playground

Expansive playground supporting athletics, football, kabaddi, volleyball, and indoor games for physical wellness.

Safe & Hygienic Campus

Secured boundary wall, RO drinking water, separate sanitation facilities for boys and girls, and Mid-Day Meal kitchen.
